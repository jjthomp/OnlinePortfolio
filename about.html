Designing the mechanical systems inside medical devices.

I'm a Biomedical Engineering graduate from UBC (2026), specialized in mechatronics. Mechanical design is my core strength — CAD, GD&T, and rapid prototyping — and I use it to build the actuators, housings, molds, and test fixtures that a device is built around, then carry that hardware through electrical integration and clinical validation myself.

I'm currently a Junior Mechanical Engineer at VRNDA Labs, where I own the mechanical design of a modular syringe pump end to end. I am also working on lab automation systems by building the fluid handling tools and tool hot swapping mechanisms. Before that, I spent close to two years at UBC's BioAugmentative Interfaces Laboratory designing and fabricating an implantable, SMA-actuated bladder management device, and a summer as a Biomedical Test Engineer at Kardium validating catheter-based ablation devices against ISO 10993.
